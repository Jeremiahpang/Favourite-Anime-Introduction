"YOU ALSO NEED TO FALL IN LOVE WITH CHUUNIBYOU!"

"YOU ALSO NEED TO FALL IN LOVE WITH CHUUNIBYOU!" is a light novel based on Huhu's "Chuunibyou Wants to Fall in Love!" And a TV animation produced by Kyoto Animation. The work tells the story of high school student Tomi Yuta who tries to abandon his past dark history of chuunibyou, But encounters a chuunibyou patient and classmate Kotori Yurikka. As the relationship between the two progressed, the two formed the club Jutou Magic Siesta Club Summer with Danio Mori Natsuki, May 7th Fennel and Tagumori Sanae.
